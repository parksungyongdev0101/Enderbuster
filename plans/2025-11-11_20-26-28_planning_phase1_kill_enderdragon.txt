To achieve the goal of killing the Ender Dragon, the following atomic action sequence must be executed. Each step is an independent action, and the necessary requirements for each step are considered.

### Atomic Action Sequence to Kill Ender Dragon

1. **Step 1:** Search Block oak_log.  
   - *Requirements:* None.  
  
2. **Step 2:** Collect Blocks oak_log.  
   - *Requirements:* Perform Step 1.  
  
3. **Step 3:** Craft Recipe wooden_pickaxe.  
   - *Requirements:* Perform Step 2.  
  
4. **Step 4:** Use Tool on wooden_pickaxe.  
   - *Requirements:* Perform Step 3.  
  
5. **Step 5:** Search Block stone.  
   - *Requirements:* Perform Step 4.  
  
6. **Step 6:** Collect Blocks stone.  
   - *Requirements:* Perform Step 5.  
  
7. **Step 7:** Craft Recipe furnace.  
   - *Requirements:* Perform Step 6.  
  
8. **Step 8:** Search Block iron_ore.  
   - *Requirements:* Perform Step 7.  
  
9. **Step 9:** Collect Blocks iron_ore.  
   - *Requirements:* Perform Step 8.  
  
10. **Step 10:** Smelt Item iron_ore in furnace.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 9 and have furnace.  
  
11. **Step 11:** Craft Recipe iron_pickaxe.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 10.  
  
12. **Step 12:** Search Block diamond_ore.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 11.  
  
13. **Step 13:** Collect Blocks diamond.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 12.  
  
14. **Step 14:** Craft Recipe diamond_pickaxe.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 13.  
  
15. **Step 15:** Search for lava pool.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 14.  
  
16. **Step 16:** Use Water Bucket to extinguish lava and make Obsidian.  
    - *Requirements:* Collect water with bucket (Step 24) and find lava.  
  
17. **Step 17:** Collect Blocks obsidian.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 16.  
  
18. **Step 18:** Build Nether Portal using obsidian.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 17.  
  
19. **Step 19:** Enter Nether through Nether Portal.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 18.  
  
20. **Step 20:** Search Block blaze_rod (from Blaze).  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 19.  
  
21. **Step 21:** Kill Blaze to collect blaze_rod.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 20.  
  
22. **Step 22:** Search Block endermen.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 21.  
  
23. **Step 23:** Kill Endermen to collect ender_pearl.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 22.  
  
24. **Step 24:** Collect water with bucket.  
    - *Requirements:* None (used for extinguishing lava).  

25. **Step 25:** Craft Recipe Eye of Ender using blaze_rod and ender_pearl.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 21 and Step 23.  
  
26. **Step 26:** Use Eye of Ender to locate End Portal.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 25.  
  
27. **Step 27:** Search Block End Portal.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 26.  
  
28. **Step 28:** Enter End Portal.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 27.  

29. **Step 29:** Search for Ender Dragon.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 28.  
  
30. **Step 30:** Kill Ender Dragon.  
    - *Requirements:* Perform Step 29.  

By following this sequence of atomic actions, you will have all necessary items and tools to kill the Ender Dragon successfully.
