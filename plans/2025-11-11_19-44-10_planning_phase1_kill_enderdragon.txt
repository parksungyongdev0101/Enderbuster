목표: kill enderdragon
=== 생성된 계획 ===

**Goal: Kill Ender Dragon**

**Preparation Stage:**

1. **Step 1:** Search for oak_log.  
   *Requirement: None*  

2. **Step 2:** Collect blocks of oak_log.  
   *Requirement: None*  

3. **Step 3:** Craft recipe wooden_pickaxe.  
   *Requirement: 3 oak_logs*  

4. **Step 4:** Use tool on wooden_pickaxe.  
   *Requirement: None*  

5. **Step 5:** Search for stone.  
   *Requirement: None*  

6. **Step 6:** Collect blocks of stone.  
   *Requirement: None*  

7. **Step 7:** Craft recipe furnace.  
   *Requirement: 8 stone*  

8. **Step 8:** Search for iron_ore.  
   *Requirement: None*  

9. **Step 9:** Collect blocks of iron_ore.  
   *Requirement: None*  

10. **Step 10:** Place furnace.  
    *Requirement: None*  

11. **Step 11:** Smelt item iron_ore.  
    *Requirement: 1 furnace + 1 iron_ore*  

12. **Step 12:** Craft recipe iron_pickaxe.  
    *Requirement: 3 iron_ingots + 2 sticks*  

13. **Step 13:** Search for diamond_ore.   
    *Requirement: None*  

14. **Step 14:** Collect blocks of diamond.  
    *Requirement: None*  

15. **Step 15:** Craft recipe diamond_pickaxe.  
    *Requirement: 3 diamonds + 2 sticks*  

**Collecting Ender Pearls & Blaze Powder:**

16. **Step 16:** Search for enderman in the Overworld.  
    *Requirement: Nighttime or Enderman spawning area*  

17. **Step 17:** Kill enderman to collect ender_pearl.  
    *Requirement: Have weapons or means to kill enderman*  

18. **Step 18:** Repeat steps 16-17 until you have at least 12 ender_perls.  
    *Requirement: Minimum 12 ender_pearl*  

19. **Step 19:** Search for blaze in the Nether.  
    *Requirement: Access to Nether*  

20. **Step 20:** Kill blaze to collect blaze_rod.  
    *Requirement: Have weapons or means to kill blaze*  

21. **Step 21:** Repeat steps 19-20 until you have at least 6 blaze_rods.  
    *Requirement: Minimum 6 blaze_rod*  

22. **Step 22:** Craft recipe blaze_powder.  
    *Requirement: 6 blaze_rods*  

23. **Step 23:** Combine blaze_powder and ender_pearl to craft eyes_of_ender.  
    *Requirement: 12 ender_perls + 6 blaze_powder*  

**Finding End Portal:**

24. **Step 24:** Search for stronghold using eyes_of_ender.  
    *Requirement: 12 eyes_of_ender*  

25. **Step 25:** Follow paths of eyes_of_ender until you locate the stronghold.  
    *Requirement: have eyes_of_ender to travel*  

26. **Step 26:** Locate the End Portal in the stronghold.  
    *Requirement: Exploring the stronghold*  

27. **Step 27:** Fill the End Portal with eyes_of_ender.   
    *Requirement: Portal frame empty + 12 eyes_of_ender*  

**Preparing for the Fight:**

28. **Step 28:** Enter the End dimension via the End Portal.  
    *Requirement: Activated End Portal*  

29. **Step 29:** Search for ender_crystals on top of obsidian pillars.  
    *Requirement: Entered the End*  

30. **Step 30:** Destroy ender_crystals using projectiles or melee attacks.  
    *Requirement: Have ranged weapons or means to destroy crystals*  

31. **Step 31:** Attack the Ender Dragon when it lands on the portal.  
    *Requirement: Must have weapons (sword, bow, etc.) and healing items*  

32. **Step 32:** Avoid or deal with the Endermen in the area.  
    *Requirement: Have strategies to mitigate damage and avoid Endermen*  

33. **Step 33:** Continue attacking the Ender Dragon until its health is depleted.  
    *Requirement: Must have sustained damage output and healing items*  

34. **Step 34:** Collect the dragon egg (optional) after defeating the Ender Dragon.  
    *Requirement: Ender Dragon defeated*  

**Goal Completion:**
- **You have successfully killed the Ender Dragon. Achievement unlocked!**
