To achieve the goal of killing the Ender Dragon in Minecraft, the following atomic action steps are required:

### Atomic Action Sequence:

1. **Search Block oak_log.**
   - *Requirement: None.*

2. **Collect Blocks oak_log.**
   - *Requirement: None.*

3. **Craft Recipe wooden_planks.**
   - *Requirement: 1 oak_log (turn into 4 wooden planks).*

4. **Craft Recipe crafting_table.**
   - *Requirement: 4 wooden_planks.*

5. **Place crafting_table.**
   - *Requirement: None.*

6. **Search Block stone.**
   - *Requirement: None.*

7. **Collect Blocks stone.**
   - *Requirement: None.*

8. **Craft Recipe furnace.**
   - *Requirement: 8 cobblestones (from collected stone).*

9. **Search Block coal or iron_ore.**
   - *Requirement: None.*

10. **Collect Blocks coal or iron_ore.**
    - *Requirement: None.*

11. **If coal: Smelt Item coal (in furnace).**
    - *Requirement: None.*
    
12. **If iron_ore: Smelt Item iron_ore.**
    - *Requirement: None.*

13. **Craft Recipe stone_pickaxe.**
    - *Requirement: 3 cobblestones and 2 wooden_planks.*

14. **Search Block iron_ore.**
    - *Requirement: None.*

15. **Collect Blocks iron_ore.**
    - *Requirement: None.*

16. **Smelt Item iron_ore.**
    - *Requirement: Furnace fuel (coal/wood).*

17. **Craft Recipe bucket.**
    - *Requirement: 3 iron ingots.*

18. **Search Block water.**
    - *Requirement: None.*

19. **Collect Blocks water with bucket.**
    - *Requirement: Bucket.*

20. **Search Block lava.**
    - *Requirement: None.*

21. **Collect Blocks lava (use bucket).**
    - *Requirement: Bucket.*

22. **Use Water Bucket to extinguish lava to create Obsidian.**
    - *Requirement: Water Bucket and Lava Source Block.*

23. **Collect Blocks obsidian.**
    - *Requirement: None (until enough blocks are collected).*

24. **Craft Recipe diamond_pickaxe.**
    - *Requirement: 3 diamonds (collected from diamond ore) and 2 sticks.*

25. **Search Block diamond_ore.**
    - *Requirement: None.*

26. **Collect Blocks diamond.**
    - *Requirement: Use diamond_pickaxe.*

27. **Collect 10 Blocks obsidian.**
    - *Requirement: You need the diamond_pickaxe to break obsidian.*

28. **Build Nether Portal.**
    - *Requirement: 10 obsidian blocks.*

29. **Enter Nether.**
    - *Requirement: Nether Portal built.*

30. **Search for blaze_spawn.**
    - *Requirement: None.*

31. **Defeat Blaze.**
    - *Requirement: Combat preparation (weapons, armor).*

32. **Collect Blaze Rod.**
    - *Requirement: Defeated Blaze.*

33. **Craft Recipe brewing_stand.**
    - *Requirement: 1 Blaze Rod and 3 cobblestones.*

34. **Search for water.**
    - *Requirement: None.*

35. **Collect water into Glass Bottles.**
    - *Requirement: Water source and Glass Bottles (crafted from sand).*

36. **Search for Nether Warts.**
    - *Requirement: None.*

37. **Collect Nether Warts.**
    - *Requirement: None.*

38. **Make potions of Strength and Health using brewing_stand.**
    - *Requirement: Glass Bottles, Nether Warts, and Blaze Powder.*

39. **Return to Overworld through Nether Portal.**
    - *Requirement: Nether Portal built.*

40. **Search Endermen (collect Ender Pearls).**
    - *Requirement: None.*

41. **Defeat Endermen to acquire Ender Pearls.**
    - *Requirement: Combat preparation (weapons, armor).*

42. **Search for Stronghold.**
    - *Requirement: Craft Eyes of Ender using Blaze Powder and Ender Pearls.*

43. **Craft Recipe Eyes of Ender.**
    - *Requirement: 1 Ender Pearl + 1 Blaze Powder (for each Eye).*

44. **Use Eyes of Ender to locate the End Portal.**
    - *Requirement: Multiple Eyes of Ender (at least 12).*

45. **Find the End Portal.**
    - *Requirement: Use Eyes in the direction of the Stronghold.*

46. **Activate End Portal.**
    - *Requirement: Place Eyes of Ender in each frame of the End Portal.*

47. **Enter End Dimension through the End Portal.**
    - *Requirement: End Portal activated.*

48. **Search for Ender Dragon.**
    - *Requirement: None.*

49. **Destroy End Crystals on Obsidian Pillars.**
    - *Requirement: Ranged weapon (Bow/Crossbow) or blocks to build up.**

50. **Engage and defeat the Ender Dragon.**
    - *Requirement: Combat preparation (weapons, arrows, armor, health potions).*

### Summary:
Follow these steps methodically to successfully prepare yourself, gather the necessary tools and resources, and finally defeat the Ender Dragon. Each step must be completed in order to progress efficiently.
